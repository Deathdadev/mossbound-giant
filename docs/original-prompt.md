# Original prompt

Verbatim prompt submitted in the Arena.ai battle that produced this repository.

```text
Create a fully realized, continuous 2D anime scene for the web. The final deliverable must be a continuous, fluid animation—NOT a slideshow, storyboard, keyframe gallery, stepped cross-fade, or static image montage. 

TECHNICAL & MOTION CONSTRAINTS (CRITICAL)
- Continuous Motion: Every second must feature full in-between frames (in-betweens / douga), fluid secondary motion, and coherent physical transitions. 
- Frame Rate & Timing: Animate with standard traditional anime timing (animating on 2s or 3s at a continuous playback of 24 fps). Do not pause or hold on still images for more than 1–2 beats of dramatic pause; all other moments must feature living motion (settling weight, drifting mist, rustling foliage, camera parallax).
- No Slideshow Formats: Do NOT deliver an image carousel, click-through sequence, panning over static stills (the "Ken Burns" effect), or AI morphs between disconnected stills. Delivery must be an actual video format (e.g., MP4, WebM) or a high-frame-rate canvas/code playback with unbroken temporal continuity.
- Model Consistency: The character and environment geometry must remain temporally stable across every frame—no morphing details, shifting scales, or visual hallucinations between frames.

ART STYLE: STUDIO GHIBLI THEATRICAL 2D
The scene must look like a scene taken directly from a finished theatrical anime film (traditional, hand-drawn aesthetic). 
- Main Subject: An original giant robot. Ancient, weathered, overgrown, kneeling alone in an abandoned forest. No humans.
- Line Art: Hand-inked anime linework with varied line weight and traditional cel ink aesthetic.
- Color & Lighting: Traditional anime cel shading with hard-edged cast shadows, paired with lush, gouache-painted background art featuring rich foliage, sunrise rim lighting, and atmospheric mist.
- Weight & Physics: Giant mecha physics—heavy momentum, lingering anticipation, dramatic follow-through, and explosive acceleration.

SCENE ACTION & TIMELINE
1. Awakening (Mist & Stillness): At golden dawn, mist drifts through an overgrown canopy. Wildflowers, moss, and roots wrap an ancient kneeling robot. A small bird hops onto its shoulder. A low pulse of light ignites deep behind its chest plate.
2. The Stand (Heavy In-Betweens): Ground rumbles; birds scatter from treetops with quick, frantic wing flaps. The robot groans and slowly heaves upright. Earth, snapping roots, and dislodged moss pour off its armor plates in cascading debris physics.
3. The Launch (Dynamic Climax): Its head snaps upward toward an opening in the canopy. Engines roar to life, blasting a shockwave ring of dust and wind outward through the trees. The robot rockets into the golden sky, leaving a curving wake of vapor and light as loose leaves spiral downward through the sunbeams.

PROHIBITED ARTIFACTS
- NO Slideshow / Stills: No sequence of still concept art, keyframe slideshows, or delayed stepping between static poses.
- NO Puppet / Cutout Rigging: No flat 2D vector bone-rigging, floating joints, or sliding SVG layers.
- NO Obvious 3D/CGI: No shiny CG plastic textures, Phong/specular digital sheen, visible wireframes, or game-engine lighting. Any 3D underlying structures must be completely rendered as hand-drawn 2D cel animation.
- NO Temporal AI Flickering: No boiling linework, shifting textures, or inconsistent frame-to-frame anatomy.

Deliver an uninterrupted, seamless sequence where every frame looks like an authentic cel from a master animator's drawing board.

Do not use ai models to generate images
```
